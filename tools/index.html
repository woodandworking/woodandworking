In the workshop

My Tools

These are some of the tools I use in my garage shop, and what I reach for them to do.

Cutting and milling

DeWalt DWE7491RS Table Saw

For ripping boards and breaking down parts to width.

Kreg Track Saw

For guided cuts in plywood and other sheet goods.

DeWalt DW735X Planer

For bringing boards to a consistent thickness.

RIDGID JP06101 6-inch Jointer

For flattening a board’s face and straightening an edge before milling it to final size.

Drilling and assembly

DeWalt Drill/Driver

For drilling pilot holes and driving screws.

Kreg Pocket-Hole Jig

For drilling angled screw holes when I use pocket-hole joinery.

Hand tools

WoodRiver No. 5½ Bench Plane

For flattening and smoothing wood by hand.

WoodRiver Block Plane

For trimming end grain, easing edges, and making small fitting adjustments.

WoodRiver Spokeshave

For shaping and refining curves.

Narex Richter Chisels

For trimming joinery and cleaning up corners and recesses.

Sanding and finishing

Mirka DEROS 6-inch Sander

For sanding furniture and cabinet surfaces. This was a splurge I found well worth it.

Earlex 5700 HVLP Sprayer

For spraying primer and paint on shop cabinetry.
