The name behind the work · DCBO

A family name
worth building on.

My great-grandfather and grandfather ran a construction business called DiSibio Builders. DiSibio Builds is a nod to that history, and a name for the furniture, woodworking, and family projects I’m making today.

The story behind the name ↗

Aspirational, but attainable.

Every year, the woodworking gets better.

I want to build beautiful things that make you think, “I’d love to make something like that.” I’m sharing the process of getting there: learning new skills, working through problems, and making each project a little better than the last.
