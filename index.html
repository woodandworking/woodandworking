Meet Steve DiSibio

Building for my family.
Learning with every project.

I’m a husband, a dad to two young boys, and a woodworker in Plano, Texas. I build in our garage, making things for our home and sharing the decisions, mistakes, and progress along the way.

The moment it felt possible

Sometimes a build changes more than a room.

Finishing and installing our hall tree was the first time I stepped back and really believed in my own skills. It made me think woodworking could become more than a hobby. That feeling is part of what keeps me building.

The name behind the work · DCBO

A family name
worth building on.

My great-grandfather and grandfather ran a construction business called DiSibio Builders. DiSibio Builds is a nod to that history, and a name for the furniture, woodworking, and family projects I’m making today.

The story behind the name ↗

Aspirational, but attainable.

Every year, the woodworking gets better.

I want to build beautiful things that make you think, “I’d love to make something like that.” I’m sharing the process of getting there: learning new skills, working through problems, and making each project a little better than the last.
