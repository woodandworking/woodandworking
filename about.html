Hi, I’m Steve.

A day job.
A growing craft.

I work in software sales enablement. Outside of work and family life, I’m making time to become a better woodworker.

Why Wood & Working?

I started this channel to document the journey: turning a hobby woodworking garage into a space where I can build furniture, and figuring out what it takes to keep making progress around a full-time job.

That means practical decisions, projects that take longer than expected, and plenty of learning along the way. I want to show the problems as well as the finished pieces.

A place to figure it out.

I’m sharing my experience as I learn. If you’re at a similar stage, or wondering how woodworking could fit into your own life, I hope these projects give you ideas and a little momentum.
