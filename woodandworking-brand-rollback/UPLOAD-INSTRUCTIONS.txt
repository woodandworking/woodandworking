WOOD & WORKING BRANDING ROLLBACK

1. Unzip and OPEN the downloaded folder.
2. In the repository ROOT, choose Add file > Upload files.
3. Upload the seven HTML files, the assets folder, and this instructions file. Do not upload the ZIP or its enclosing folder.
4. Commit changes. Keep your existing CNAME file (woodandworking.com).
5. After publishing finishes, refresh with Command + Shift + R.

The assets folder contains BOTH stylesheets and BOTH Wood & Working logos.
No DNS, hosting, or email settings need changing.
Old social URLs have been removed until replacement URLs are supplied.
